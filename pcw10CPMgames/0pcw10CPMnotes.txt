Terminal: vt52 sometimes GEMDOS extentions required, Sometimes GEMDOS extentions won't work (^E instead of ^H^J for clear screen)

CPMtools diskdef:

diskdef pcw
  seclen 512
  tracks 40
  sectrk 9
  blocksize 1024
  maxdir 64
  skew 1
  boottrk 1
  os 3
end

Workflow: Load .DSK boot disk into HxC, export as .IMG, edit with CPMtoolsgui, in HxC 'Load Raw image' with above values, export .IMD.

Autostart: CPM3, so support for SUBMIT PROFILE built in.

MAME286 command line:
